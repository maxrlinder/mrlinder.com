ABOUT // STOCKHOLM, SWEDEN

Greetings!

My name is Max, and I’m an engineer and researcher based in Stockholm.

Jump to: Background Studies Current work

Background

I grew up playing football for over a decade before high school, then went on to sail catamarans. Eventually, I sailed at KSSS and worked at KSSS summer camps for children. In 2020, I won silver in the Swedish championship for the F18 class of catamarans. My favourite place on Earth is the archipelago, where I still spend many weeks a year either relaxing or doing activities such as participating in the Stockholm Archipelago Raid.

I have always been curious about how and why things work. Paired with an interest in maths and physics, it has always been a natural choice for me to go down that path, ever since I had the chance to choose my own specialisation—starting with the Natural Science Programme at Kungsholmen for my secondary education. Before starting university, I completed one year of military service as a Jägare at K3, from which I have almost only good memories, despite being constantly tired, cold, and hungry (Ranger or Chasseur equivalent).

Studies

After my “gap year”, I began studying Engineering Physics at KTH, which gathers students who are really interested in and/or have an aptitude for physics and maths. Some peers know from the start that they will go into a PhD, finance, or some other field, while others, like myself, only knew that they had a passion for the subjects taught. Despite starting during peak COVID, I had the luck of finding a wonderful set of people who shared more than a few niche interests.

It’s possible that the first half of the 2020s has been the most technologically transformative five years in the history of civilisation (yet), thanks to—or because of—AI. This has been even more apparent as a STEM student aiming to choose a path for the future. In 2022, I wanted to take my studies to the next level and therefore made an effort to apply for and get into a full-year exchange programme at l’X. I saw promise in ML and therefore pivoted towards Applied Mathematics for Data Science for my master’s. After I had already applied, the release of ChatGPT changed machine learning and deep learning from sometimes useful tools and research domains into something that would turn society on its head. At this pivotal point in time, I had a solid background in computer science, physics, and maths, and a study plan filled to the brim with elective courses in deep learning ahead of me.

During my 2023/24 academic year at École Polytechnique, I mainly followed its specialisation track in applied mathematics, focusing on statistical learning and the mathematical foundations of deep learning, as well as more practical courses in reinforcement learning and deep learning from the computer science department. Besides my main studies, I joined the rowing team and took courses in Management and History. The most unique and enjoyable part of the whole experience was living, doing sports, and taking courses in the fully French curriculum with the Francophones. As part of the curriculum, I also did a stage (internship) at Publicis’ headquarters in Paris, where I trained a model for virtual try-on for clothes. In short, I trained adapters for the open-source image model SDXL. As I learned in the process, the most crucial part of training an AI model is augmenting the data, and during the process I learned to build a pipeline end to end: from formatting the data and using other AI models to label it, to training a model on those labels to predict the image corresponding to specifications for garment length, type, dimensions, and more.

Current work

I did my master’s thesis (2025) at Ericsson, at the newly formed System Comprehension Lab, which at the time was one of very few labs in the Nordics conducting deep research into LLMs. I researched a new way of performing vocabulary expansion of LLMs, i.e. adding tokens after the model had already been trained. This led me down a path of working with tokenisers, self-distillation, and building new, unique training code from scratch. I finished my thesis in August 2025 and defended it in January 2026.

Since September 2025, I have worked full-time as an AI engineer and researcher at Ericsson, building AI that can develop 5G/6G. Because the tech stack is about as complicated as imaginable—from custom hardware and memory management to custom code that runs on it—there is a need to gather all the knowledge in one place and train AI that can become an expert in the domain. This is especially true since all the material is closed source and quite alien to any LLM trained on data from the public domain. The past year has also seen a vast change in what AI can do and how it is used. In the time between my internship and thesis, AI went from being a tool that could help build one function at a time to something that can implement higher-level features. This has not only changed how I work, but also shifted how the AI one builds should be trained. During the past year, I have mainly focused on building data and training pipelines, as well as data versioning, with an emphasis on reproducibility and modularity. I really enjoy the privilege of working with what I see as both my interest and cutting-edge hardware and software. I still find great pleasure in understanding how and why things work. Therefore, gaining an intuition for AI is not only my most important knowledge as an engineer, but also something that gives me satisfaction.

Recently, I have been building smaller RL models to beat my family at random social card games (hidden-information, non-Nash-equilibrium games, most of them). I hope to add details and the ability to play against them on this website soon as well.

/ Max
