    AT THIS TABLE

    How to play Rummy

    Be the first to empty your hand. Every turn has three parts:

    1. Draw one. Take the top card from the stock or the discard pile.
    2. Make your melds. A set is 3–4 of the same rank in different suits. A run is 3–13 consecutive cards of the same suit. Runs can wrap: Q–K–A and K–A–2 both work.
    3. Discard one. Finish your turn by discarding. Keep at least one card after melding: you must discard your last card to win.

    Thirty points to open

    Your first melds must total at least 30 points in one turn. You can lay them one at a time; once you start, finish the opening before discarding. After opening, you can also add cards to anyone’s melds.

    Counting the cards

    Number cards count their value; J, Q and K count 10. Aces count 11, except at the start of a short run (A–2–3), where they count 1. Aces left in your hand always cost 11.

    At the end

    The winner gains the other players’ hand values; each other player loses their own. Opening and going out in one turn doubles the scores if the table was empty at the start of that turn: Rummy!

    Drawing the last stock card ends the round after that turn. If nobody goes out, everyone loses their hand value. A 200-turn limit ends long discard loops the same way.
