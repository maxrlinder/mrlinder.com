SWEDISH CARD GAME

Plump.

Predict exactly how many tricks you will win. Get it right to score; miss by one and you still get nothing.

  1. Everyone bids from 0 up to the number of cards in hand.
  2. The final bid may not make the table total equal the hand size.
  3. Follow the led suit when you can. There is no trump suit.
  4. The highest card in the led suit wins and leads the next trick.
  5. A correct bid scores 10 + bid. A correct zero scores 05. A miss scores 00.

    UNDER THE HOOD

    How the agent was trained.

    The AI you play is a small transformer, 8.6M parameters, that learned Plump by playing against itself: about a month on one Mac GPU and roughly 3.4 billion decisions. It only sees what you see, which is its own hand and the public table.

    01 · ARCHITECTURE

    Every card and every action is a token.

    3P·3Ctable A♠9♥4♦your hand BID 1BID 0BID 1bids A♠7♠2♠WINtrick 1 ?next

    A round is written down like a sentence: the table, the cards in your hand, each bid, each card played and who won each trick. A 16-layer causal transformer reads that sequence and predicts the next token, which is either a bid or one of the legal cards.

    Because the model is autoregressive, every move just appends one token. In self-play it keeps a key-value cache and decodes one token per move for hundreds of games at once. In training, a single forward and backward pass over a finished game updates every decision in it.

    02 · OBJECTIVE

    Beat the table average.

    The reward is your round score minus the average opponent score. Every seat is played by the same network, and it is trained with PPO. Actions that ended better than expected become more likely and worse ones less likely. Each step is clipped so the policy changes gradually, and an entropy floor keeps it exploring.

    The “expected” score comes from an oracle critic, a second transformer that sees every hand. It only exists during training, to cut the noise in the reward signal. Side heads also learn to predict opponents’ suits, card ranges and trick counts. Those are the Actor beliefs you can switch on in the game.

    1. WIDER MODEL · 6 LAYERS × 384 PPO from scratch 100,500 updates · ~24 days
    2. DISTILLATION Copied into a deeper net 16 layers × 240. The student plays and the wider model labels every move · 5,600 updates
    3. DEEPER MODEL · 16 LAYERS × 240 PPO self-play continues 696 games per update · checkpoint 34,800 is live

    x-axis: training updates. The deeper model continues from update 5,600 of the distillation. The round robin covers checkpoints up to 27,700.
