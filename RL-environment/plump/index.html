SWEDISH CARD GAME

Plump.

Predict exactly how many tricks you will win. Get it right to score; miss by one and you still get nothing.

  1. Everyone bids from 0 up to the number of cards in hand.
  2. The final bid may not make the table total equal the hand size.
  3. Follow the led suit when you can. There is no trump suit.
  4. The highest card in the led suit wins and leads the next trick.
  5. A correct bid scores 10 + bid. A correct zero scores 05. A miss scores 00.
